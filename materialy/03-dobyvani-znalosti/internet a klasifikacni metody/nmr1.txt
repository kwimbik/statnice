    Aleatoric and Epistemic Uncertainty

 Aleatoric uncertainty: randomness conveyed by random variables

    · inherent in the systems/processes described by them

 Epistemic uncertainty: arises from lack of knowledge

    · 1. insufficient data, 2. incomplete models (differing from reality)

 Aleatoric irreducible, epistemic reducible by more knowledge:

    · more data, better generalizing models, additional expert judgement
